Who we are

Built for the long series

We're not chasing one good map. Drakthys runs a full-time practice block, weekly VOD review and a scouting pipeline built to find players who win rounds on read, not on luck.

Every set piece is drilled before it's played live. Every loss gets a breakdown, not an excuse. That's the standard the org was built on, and it's the only one we keep.
